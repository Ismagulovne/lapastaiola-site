Discover the ultimate pasta-making experience

Dive into the art of authentic pasta making. Our hands-on classes take you through the heart of Italian cuisine, where you'll learn to make exquisite pasta from scratch.

Fresh Pasta Making Class · $150.00 per person · about 2–3 hours · up to 6 guests

Request a classCall +1 (970) 975-0858

Why choose us

  • Authentic Italian expertise. Learn techniques passed down through generations.
  • Hands-on learning. Every step, from kneading and rolling to shaping and cooking.
  • Fresh, high-quality ingredients. All ingredients and tools are provided.
  • Fun and friendly. Welcoming for complete beginners and experienced cooks alike.

Class details

  • Classes last 2 to 3 hours and start with Italian finger foods. Refreshments are served throughout.
  • Please bring your own reusable containers to take your pasta home.
  • Each class is limited to 6 attendees, and everyone needs their own paid spot.
  • Private classes at your home are available at an additional cost.
  • We can accommodate dietary requirements; tell us when you book.

Class options

  • Introduction to pasta making: dough basics, traditional shapes and classic sauces.
  • Advanced pasta techniques: intricate shapes and new recipes.
  • Custom private classes: for special occasions and group events.

Kids' pasta fun

Our pasta room is a great place for children to discover cooking. Children under 6 join for free; kids aged 6 to 14 get a discounted rate. Email info@lapastaiola.co for details.
