Fresh pasta artisans in the Roaring Fork Valley

Aina and Marco Baghetti are excited to bring La Pastaiola's artisanal Italian fresh pasta and sauces to the Roaring Fork Valley.

Marco is from Naples, Italy. His childhood memories are mostly of being in the kitchen with his nonna and mother, watching them cook, debating the best ingredients and deciding the day's menu for the family. Holidays are his best memories, with the traditional Neapolitan food of the festive season. Every Easter, Marco and his mom still cook together over video call.

Aina is from Almaty, Kazakhstan, and grew up in a large family of seven children where experimenting with family meals was always fun. She built a career in hospitality and mastered the art of pasta making in Italy, to create a product made with skill, love and tradition.

Marco and Aina met while working at Il Gattopardo restaurant in New York City. They share a passion for Italian food and exceptional service. Years of experience in food and wine, restaurant management and catering led them to open an artisanal pastificio in the Roaring Fork Valley.

They have two sons, Matteo and Stefano, to whom they hope to pass on their passion.
