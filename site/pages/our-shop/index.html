Our shop

It's not just a store to us. It is our lifestyle, where we come from, and what we hold most important.

Italian food culture is about sharing a meal with family. Meals are made with attention, care and pride, using only fresh, quality ingredients. We have taken that tradition, along with our experience in the industry, to create a place where friends and family can shop for the freshest pasta and Italian imported goods.

At La Pastaiola we are an authentic pastificio, making fresh pasta from scratch in our kitchen with premium ingredients and traditional Italian recipes. Our bronze extruding dies give the pasta a rough, porous texture that absorbs the sauce when you finish the dish.

In the shop you'll find fresh pasta, sauces made in-house and carefully selected artisanal products made in Italy. We also create custom Italian menus for catering.
